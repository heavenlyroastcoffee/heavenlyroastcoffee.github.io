What we serve

Handcrafted espresso drinks, ceremonial-grade matcha, chai and lemonade, made to order on site. Every drink can be hot or iced, and dairy-free milk is available. Browse the drink menu.

Events we cover

Wedding coffee bars for cocktail hour or dessert, corporate events and client meetings, private parties like birthdays and baby showers, and community events such as church gatherings, markets and 5Ks. Want matcha front and center? See our matcha bar.

How booking works

Share your date, venue or city, estimated guest count and the time you want drinks served. The cart needs a flat area of about six by six feet, access to power and venue approval. Popular dates go early, so read how far in advance to book.
